WHICH PLOT I WOULD PUBLISH, AND WHY
(Species-level ordination, notebook 03, Exercise 1, Step 2)
Datasets and numbers: 471 samples, 220 species, community = lactobacillus
if Lactobacillus fraction >= 0.5 else anaerobe (data/my_sample_groups.csv).
All numbers come from the same microViz pipeline as the PNGs in this folder
and are also recorded in ordination_summary.csv.

THE CHOICE: Aitchison (CLR transform + Euclidean distance) ordination.
Publish the Aitchison PCoA coloured by community as the main figure, with its
scree plot beside it. Put the three-distance comparison
(03_three_distances_scree.png) in the supplement as the evidence for the choice.

THREE REASONS, EACH WITH A NUMBER

1. It is the honest distance for compositional data.
   Species counts are shares of a fixed sequencing budget, so "this species is
   rarer" can just mean "other species got more of the budget".
   Aitchison (CLR + Euclidean) is built for that; Bray-Curtis inherits the
   problem and Jaccard sidesteps it only by discarding abundance entirely.

2. It shows the structure that is actually there, better than the others:
   - most variation on axis 1:     31.7%  (Bray 27.5%, Jaccard 17.6%)
   - axis 1 vs lacto_fraction:     |r| = 0.88  (Bray 0.29, Jaccard 0.75)
   - PERMANOVA R2 for community:   0.207, p = 0.001  (Bray 0.200, Jaccard 0.095)
   Its scree plot has one tall bar, so the 2D view published carries most of
   the signal. Jaccard's scree spreads variation over many small bars.

3. It survives the reviewer check: axis 1 is NOT tracking library size
   (|r| = 0.24), so the ordination is not an artefact of sequencing depth.

CAVEATS (recorded for the "Choices made" section)
- ~89% of the species table is zeros. CLR cannot take log(0), so zeros must be
  handled somehow. The published figures use microViz tax_transform("clr"),
  which handles zeros internally. A hand-rolled check with a +0.5 pseudocount
  gave similar conclusions but slightly different percentages (e.g. axis 1
  31.7% vs 27.5%) - that is the pseudocount fudge showing up.
- PCoA axis signs are arbitrary: a rerun can flip left/right or
  positive/negative. Compare magnitudes (|r|, R2), not signs.

ALTERNATIVES NOT CHOSEN (one line each, for "Choices made")
- Bray-Curtis: the microbiome default, but its abundances are confounded by
  compositionality, and the community split is smeared over two axes
  (27.5% / 21.3%).
- Jaccard (presence/absence): discards abundance, so it separates the community
  groups least (R2 = 0.095) and no single axis carries the signal.
- Untransformed Euclidean on counts: dominated by the most abundant species,
  effectively redundant with the above; not shown.
